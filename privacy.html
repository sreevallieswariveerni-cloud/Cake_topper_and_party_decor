01

Introduction

At Party Pop, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website or enquire about our products and services.

02

Information We Collect

When you use our website or submit an enquiry, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Event and celebration details
  • Design preferences and requirements
  • Information submitted through enquiry or upload forms
03

How We Use Your Information

The information you provide may be used to understand your requirements and communicate with you regarding your enquiry or requested service.

  • To respond to your enquiries
  • To understand your customization requirements
  • To provide information about our products and services
  • To improve our website and user experience
04

Design & Uploaded Information

If you upload a design, reference image or other material through our website, we use that information only for the purpose for which it was submitted, such as understanding and discussing your customization requirements.

05

Information Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to respond to your request, provide a service, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

07

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and improve your browsing experience. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

08

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links or integrations provided by third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we recommend reviewing those policies when using their services.

09

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information. However, some information may be necessary for us to respond to an enquiry or provide a requested service.

10

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page.

11

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact us through our Contact or Enquire Now page.
